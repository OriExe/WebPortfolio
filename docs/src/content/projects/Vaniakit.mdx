VaniaKit is a Metroidvania toolkit that I made in Unity which uses modern systems present in Unity 6 such as the new input system. It's built to help make building Metroidvania games easier for students and less experienced programmers. It was part of my final year project where I scored over 70%. I focused on the coding while sprites and sounds were sourced from external sources.
