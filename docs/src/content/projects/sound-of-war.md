# Sound of war 
An fps game I made with my team at the University of Westminster. It's a first person shooter inspired by cod zombies where you kill tons of enemies to progress through rounds and fight harder enemies that spawn in higher frequencies. The player doesn't have a lot of ammo in their disposal and will feel encouraged to pick up and use more weapons. The game was made in a 10 week time frame with a 3 week break in the middle. I earned free gift cards at the end of development
